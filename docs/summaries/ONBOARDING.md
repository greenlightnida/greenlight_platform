# Top Bins Team Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the Top Bins platform! This guide will help you get started as a team member for the 2025–2026 year.

## 1. Logging In
- Go to the Top Bins portal URL (provided by your admin).
- Use your email and password to log in. If you don't have an account, contact your admin to be invited.

## 2. Uploading Media
- Click the "Upload" button or drag-and-drop files into the uploader.
- You can upload multiple files at once (batch upload recommended: 100–200 files per batch).
- Wait for the progress bar to complete. You'll see a summary of successes and any errors.

## 3. Using the Storage Dashboard
- Go to the "Settings" or "Admin" section and open the Storage Dashboard.
- View current storage usage and free space.
- Use the "Clean Up Old Media" button to delete test or outdated files (admin only).
- Download a usage report for your records.

## 4. Support & Best Practices
- If you have issues, check the FAQ or contact your admin.
- Only delete media if you are an admin or have permission.
- Upload in batches for best performance.
- Keep your login credentials secure.

## 5. Getting Help
- For technical support, email nida@greenlight.live or your team admin.
- For urgent issues, use the team Slack channel or group chat.

Welcome aboard! Let's make this year a success.
