# 🛡️ TEAM INTEGRATION MANDATE

**Issued:** 2025-07-09
**Applies to:** Frontend Manager, Backend Manager, Product Manager, and their teams

---

## 🎯 **Mandate Overview**

To ensure the Greenlight Platform delivers a seamless, high-quality experience, the Frontend Manager, Backend Manager, and Product Manager are required to:
- Maintain continuous alignment on system architecture, features, and priorities
- Collaborate on all integration points and cross-functional workflows
- Proactively identify and resolve blockers, risks, and inefficiencies
- Document and review all major decisions and protocols

---

## 🔗 **Integration Protocol**

### 1. **Weekly Sync Meetings**
- **Participants:** Frontend Manager, Backend Manager, Product Manager (required)
- **Agenda:**
  - Review current sprint/milestone progress
  - Discuss integration points and upcoming changes
  - Identify blockers, dependencies, and cross-team needs
  - Align on priorities and success criteria
- **Action Items:** Documented and tracked in the shared roadmap

### 2. **System Knowledge Base**
- **Central Documentation:**
  - System architecture diagrams
  - API contracts and data flows
  - Component/service registry and ownership
  - Shared protocols (auth, error handling, logging, etc.)
- **Responsibility:** All managers must contribute and review updates

### 3. **Joint Planning & Review**
- **Milestone Reviews:** Jointly review integration quality, system health, and user impact at the end of each milestone
- **Pre-Implementation Reviews:** All major API/interface changes require sign-off from all three managers

### 4. **Transparent Communication**
- **Channels:** Shared Slack/Teams channels and email threads for all system-level topics
- **Decision Logs:** All architectural/protocol decisions are logged and accessible

### 5. **Continuous Improvement**
- **Retrospectives:** Monthly cross-functional retrospectives to review what’s working, what’s not, and action items for improvement
- **Training:** Regular workshops and onboarding for new protocols, tools, and best practices

---

## 📋 **Team Integration Checklist**

### **Weekly**
- [ ] Attend and participate in sync meeting
- [ ] Review and update shared documentation
- [ ] Identify and communicate blockers/dependencies
- [ ] Align on priorities and deliverables

### **Per Milestone**
- [ ] Conduct joint milestone review
- [ ] Review and sign off on integration/API changes
- [ ] Update system health and integration dashboards

### **Ongoing**
- [ ] Maintain up-to-date documentation
- [ ] Log all major decisions
- [ ] Participate in retrospectives and implement improvements
- [ ] Ensure all new team members are onboarded to protocols

---

## 🏆 **Success Criteria**
- System-level integration issues are proactively identified and resolved
- All managers are aware of and aligned on current priorities and changes
- Documentation is always current and accessible
- Cross-team blockers are addressed within one business day
- Continuous improvement is demonstrated in workflow efficiency and product quality

---

**This mandate is effective immediately and is required for all current and future projects. Non-compliance will be escalated to executive leadership.**
