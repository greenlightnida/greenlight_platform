- **Policy Updates**: Update and improve governance policies
- **Audit Preparation**: Prepare for upcoming audits and reviews

#### **Priority Calculation**
- **Impact Factor**: 0.4 (Business impact and user experience)
- **Urgency Factor**: 0.3 (Time sensitivity and deadlines)
- **Effort Factor**: 0.2 (Required effort and complexity)
- **Risk Factor**: 0.1 (Associated risks and potential issues)

#### **Resource Allocation**
- **Effort Estimation**: Hours required to complete the docket
- **Complexity Assessment**: Low, medium, or high complexity
- **Required Skills**: Technical skills needed for completion
- **Dependencies**: System access and tools required

#### **Timeline Estimation**
- **Estimated Days**: Calculated based on effort and complexity
- **Start Date**: When work can begin
- **End Date**: Expected completion date
- **Milestones**: Key milestones and checkpoints

#### **Risk Assessment**
- **Technical Risk**: Risk of technical issues during implementation
- **Operational Risk**: Risk of operational disruption
- **Business Risk**: Risk of business impact
- **Overall Risk**: Combined risk assessment

### **Assignment Logic**

#### **Manager Assignment**
- **Backend Manager**: Dependency, compilation, server issues
- **Frontend Manager**: UI, component, user experience issues
- **API Manager**: Performance, optimization, integration issues
- **Holon Manager**: Communication, coordination, system issues
- **Governance Manager**: Security, compliance, policy issues

#### **Scheduling Logic**
- **Priority-Based**: Higher priority dockets scheduled first
- **Resource-Aware**: Considers available resources and capacity
- **Dependency-Aware**: Considers dependencies between dockets
- **Risk-Adjusted**: Adjusts scheduling based on risk assessment
